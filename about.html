Philosophy

Grounded. Reliable. Local.

AnchorPoint IT started because small businesses, nonprofits, and churches were being underserved — stuck with either consumer-grade gear or overpriced MSP contracts that didn't fit their actual needs.

The anchor in our name isn't just decoration. It's a promise: to be a stable, grounded presence for your organization's technology. No upselling. No unnecessary complexity. Just honest advice and solid work.

Based in Leavenworth, KS, we serve the local community directly — someone who knows your building, your team, and your constraints.
