Core Solutions for Modern Infrastructure

We don't just "fix Wi-Fi." We engineer environments that are secure, segmented, and sustainable. Our core competencies include:

  • Network Design & Implementation: We specialize in professional-grade ecosystems like Ubiquiti UniFi. By moving away from standard ISP-provided routers and implementing managed switches and dedicated access points, we eliminate dead zones and bottlenecks.
  • Logical Segmentation (VLANs): In an age of IoT (Internet of Things) vulnerabilities, security is paramount. We design networks that separate your private data from your smart devices and guest traffic, ensuring that a compromised light bulb doesn't lead to a compromised bank account.
  • Smart Home & Automation Consulting: For those looking to go beyond basic voice assistants, we provide expertise in self-hosted platforms like Home Assistant. We focus on local control and privacy, ensuring your home remains functional even if your internet connection drops.
  • Remote Work & Connectivity: For the professional working from home, we implement VPN solutions and SD-WAN configurations that prioritize your critical traffic, ensuring your video conferences and data transfers are never interrupted.
