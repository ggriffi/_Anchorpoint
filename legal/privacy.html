Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2, 2026

AnchorPoint IT is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and organization name when you submit a consultation request
  • Details about your IT needs provided through our contact form
  • Your public IP address when using our network diagnostic tools, processed temporarily to calculate network pathing and latency

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our consultation form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule a consultation
  • Communicate with you about services relevant to your request

IP address data collected through diagnostic tools is used only for the technical purpose of providing that diagnostic and is not stored beyond the session.

3. Data Protection

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Our diagnostic tools use redaction logic to ensure internal infrastructure addresses are never exposed in public-facing reports.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses Cloudflare Turnstile for spam protection on our contact form. Cloudflare's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

Consultation request data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the requested service or as required by applicable law.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you by contacting us directly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will note the effective date of any changes at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For questions or requests regarding your data, contact us at support@anchorpoint-it.com.
