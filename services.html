Services

Infrastructure

Network Infrastructure

VLAN Segmentation

Designing secure, high-performance networks with proper traffic isolation. Keep guest traffic, staff devices, and sensitive systems cleanly separated.

Security

Security & Firewalls

Security isn't something I bolt on at the end — it's in every build from the start. If your network has gaps, I'll find them before someone else does.

  • Firewall Configuration: Proper rule sets, zone policies, and traffic inspection that match your actual threat profile.
  • Endpoint Security: Policies and tooling to protect workstations, servers, and mobile devices connecting to your network.
  • Access Control: Role-based network access so the right people reach the right resources — and nothing more.
  • Security Audits: Identifying gaps and vulnerabilities in your existing infrastructure before they become incidents.
Support

Managed IT Support

Day-to-day issues or long-term planning — I'm the same person either way. You call me, not a ticket queue. I know your setup and I'm not starting from scratch every time something breaks.

  • Tech Planning: Honest advice on what to upgrade, when to upgrade it, and what's not worth spending on yet.
  • Vendor Coordination: I deal with ISPs, hardware vendors, and software providers so you don't have to.
  • Documentation: Clean, maintained network docs so anyone working on your system actually knows what they're looking at.
